About the Podcast

Who are we?

Anna & Marla. Sisters. Roommates. Besties. Co-Hosts.

What is going on?

We're recording our conversations and sharing them with you every Sunday.

Why?

The sh*t we say needs to be documented for study. More importantly, we want to make you laugh or giggle or something.
